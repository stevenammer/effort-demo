# Exact revision proposals and a separate choice

A saved correction or supplied transcript can be prepared as an ordinary reply proposal. Preparation copies that revision's complete exact string and preserves its original parent, Attention Span, revision ID and revision checksum. It doesn't create an intent event or change NOW. Choosing a quote from the prepared reply is a second explicit action, with offsets into that reply's bytes rather than the original post.

The proposal is a new immutable capture with `sourceRevisionId`, `sourceRevisionHash` and a metadata seal. Its identity is deterministic from the saved revision ID and checksum, independent of workspace reset epochs. Concurrent requests, failed-readback retries and preparation after backup restoration reuse one accepted capture. Canonical identity and metadata validation reject substituted or mixed provenance fields. Capacity failure refuses preparation without removing earlier writing.

Each revision history page offers preparation for the exact row selected. Earlier revisions are accessible through pagination, even when a newer edit has replaced the EF projection. A newly saved edit doesn't retarget a prepared proposal, its chosen quote, its commitment or a completion receipt. An explicitly selected supplied transcript retains its text-only provenance even when an authored correction takes precedence in EF. A verified link doesn't establish authorship, truth or verified audio.

The proposal's original dialog shows its exact saved revision and original source separately, with the revision record available as a download. Damaged or missing lineage preserves the proposal's own exact writing and shows recovery instead of verified provenance. If revision storage is unavailable, lineage is unknown and a complete backup refuses omission; primary capture and existing chosen work stay usable. A deleted lineage record receives a portable derivation recovery copy. A new proposal requires both the selected revision and a verified original.

Preparation preserves later navigation and doesn't reopen an original dialog that was dismissed while it was pending. A workspace reset fences old writes and result displays. Preparing is explicit; no helper, provider, external message, repository executor or automatic commitment runs.

Schema11 fences older running clients from the new strict capture format. Backup13 imports1..12 and carries source-revision proposals, their source histories, exact choices and raw recovery. An older compatible snapshot doesn't replace current prepared writing or chosen work. The source-revision table and its formats are otherwise unchanged from the preceding release.

Acceptance includes concurrent/recovered retry identity, exact CRLF strings, tampered metadata, corrupted or deleted lineage, optional-store failure, full capture capacity, earlier-history pagination, distinct transcript provenance, explicit own-copy quote choice, unchanged NOW after new edits, pending navigation/reset, backup13/old12 and offline reopen.
